## Why this is a real closed loop (not a relabel)

- `task_env.py` calls `mj_contactForce()` on the bottle geom **every simulation step** and
  P-regulates `reach_offset` to a setpoint — **never writes qpos**.
- **Sensor-dependent (audit proof):** `audit.py` replaces `_measure_bottle_contact` with
  `lambda: 0.0`. Blind run → `reach_offset_final = 0.0`. Sighted run → `reach_offset_final = −0.04`.
  A cosmetic loop is unaffected by this; ours changes measurably.
- Freejoint bottle and hinge door move **only under contact forces** — nothing is teleported.
- `dex_grasp.py` reads live `touch_forces` from 3 fingertip touch sensors **every step** and
  escalates grip within **2 steps (4 ms)** on slip detection.
